01 / The Core Problem & Thesis

The attention crisis is an architectural failure, not personal weakness.

The consumer software industry underwent a catastrophic shift: platforms abandoned serving the user as customer, turning the user's attention into the harvested product. Every infinite scroll, automated push alert, and outrage-amplifying recommender system was deliberately engineered to induce trance states.

Mainstream Extractive Paradigm

How mainstream apps trap attention

  • Infinite scroll & variable dopamine: Slot-machine feed mechanics prevent cognitive completion and degrade executive function.
  • Surveillance monetization: Behavioral telemetry silently captures user interactions to maximize ad revenue and auction attention.
  • Algorithmic outrage velocity: Recommenders boost divisive, high-arousal content over deliberate, reflective communication.
  • Proprietary silos: Notes, conversations, and personal memories are locked behind walled gardens to force retention.
The Resistance Labs Standard

How we build for human wellness

  • Bounded sessions & finite rooms: Software that respects human boundaries. Applications designed to be closed so users can live offline.
  • Zero ad-tech & local-first data: You are the customer, not the product. User data lives on your disk, in open standards (SQLite, Markdown).
  • Synchronous human scale: Capped communities (e.g. 1,000 members) and intentional cadences that foster real relationships.
  • Private intelligence assistance: Sovereign reasoning tools deployed strictly on the user's side to synthesize knowledge and maintain civil discourse.

Suntown

A Moderated Daily Social Room for Slower Public Conversation

An online gathering space engineered against 24/7 doomscrolling. Suntown operates on a strict daily ritual: it opens for a 4-hour Golden Hour (14:00–18:00 UTC) and closes completely for the remaining twenty hours to make room for offline life.

The Golden Hour Cadence

Opens strictly from 14:00 to 18:00 UTC. When the town closes, the town sleeps. No midnight push notifications.

Capped 1,000-Seat Cohorts

Early cohorts are capped at 1,000 people so names and conversations remain human-scale instead of anonymous mobs.

Original Writing & Daily Themes

Centered on deliberate, longer-form daily writing topics rather than reactive hot takes or meme recycling.

Claude-Powered Admissions Screening

Prospective members submit written responses evaluated using Claude models to determine whether they meet town customs and original writing commitments.

03 / Applied AI & Engineering Architecture

Intelligence on the user’s side of the glass.

Artificial intelligence should not be an engine for micro-targeted advertising. Resistance Labs deploys private intelligence and local-first models to put high-order reasoning directly into the hands of individual humans.

Deep Semantic Synthesis

ATLAS:Face leverages high-capacity contextual reasoning to parse entire books, academic papers, and personal notes simultaneously. Instead of superficial keyword matching, users get genuine conceptual cross-synthesis across years of personal research.

Capability: Multi-Document Knowledge Graphs

Admissions Screening & Civic Stewardship

In Suntown, maintaining a high-trust, human-scale forum begins at intake. We use Claude models to evaluate prospective member application responses against community requirements and original writing commitments, alongside context-aware assistance to help community stewards de-escalate tension.

Capability: Claude-Powered Intake & Civic Stewardship

Client-Side Sovereignty

Our technical covenant: zero training on user notes, zero data brokering, zero profiling. Model queries are processed on user demand via sovereign endpoints, with zero retention on application servers and zero telemetry leakage.

Guarantee: Zero User Data Brokering
